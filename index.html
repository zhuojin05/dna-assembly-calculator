🧬 DNA Assembly Protocol & Smart Dilution Guide

1. Supported Kits & Custom Scale

Use the Kit / Premix Preset menu to switch between 5X In-Fusion (standard 1/5 volume or half-reaction 1/10 volume), 2X NEBuilder HiFi (1/2 volume), 2X Gibson Assembly (1/2 volume), or any custom total & premix volume. With Auto Ratio enabled, scaling total volume automatically scales premix volume; typing directly into Premix Vol switches to Fixed mode.

2. Core Stoichiometric Principle

  • Required Insert Mass: M_insert = M_vector × MolarRatio × (Length_insert / Length_vector)
  • Raw Stock Volume: V_raw = M_insert / C_stock
  • Vector Pipetting Volume: V_vector = M_vector / C_vector (computed directly from your desired linearized vector mass in ng).

3. Constrained Smart Dilution Solver

High-concentration or short PCR amplicons often require sub-pipettable raw volumes (< V_min). Naively diluting every insert 1:10 can overflow the remaining water+insert reaction budget when paired with large or dilute fragments.

  • The solver searches candidate dilutions D ∈ {10, 5, 6, 4, 8, 2, 20, 50, 100} per insert.
  • It prefers 1:10 whenever all inserts fit comfortably below the reaction budget, and automatically steps down (e.g. 1:5, 1:6) or up (1:20, 1:50, 1:100) to keep every pipetted volume ≥ Vmin within budget.
  • Table 1 consolidates identical insert dilutions across your batch with configurable dead-volume excess (default 20%).

4. SnapGene Cloning History Importer

Drop a ligated/assembled SnapGene .dna product file directly onto the importer bar. The zero-server parser reads its XZ-compressed HistoryTree to recover restriction-cleaved vector length (accounting for excised stuffer), insert sizes, PCR primers, and exact 5'→3' homology overlaps.
